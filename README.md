# WayLand
Temptation is like a weed. Once it appears in the garden, it spreads until every fruit is tainted. And such happened to civilization. Humans stopped believing in God. The people murdered, raped, whored, and lied. And God was furious. Thus, God sent a flood of water to wipe out all living creatures. However, God started to have misgivings about his decision. Maybe there was still hope for humanity. So, God proclaimed never to bring harm upon any living creature on Earth. After the tides had gone down, creating islands scattered throughout the earth, God restored all living creatures, other than humans, just as he had on the sixth day of creation, and he saw that it was good... except for one creature, the serpent. The serpent was cunning. God was unable to curse the serpent for its wrongdoings in Eden, and thus the serpent walked on four legs. Soon, the serpent collected all creatures and brought them under his influence. So, God created one human and told them to find the serpent and subdue it, for it had the power to turn all living creatures on Earth away from God. If the human passed this “test of humanity”, God would grant them a spouse with whom to repopulate the earth.

WayLand is a 2D top-down exploration game about sailing through the world and fighting Satan. It is built with Wispy Engine and runs on C++.

The first screen is the main menu. To enter play mode, press 'F'. Once in play mode, use the arrow keys to move the player around. To enable debug mode, press '`'.

As of right now, basic collision handling is developed, but it is planned to implement world generation and gameplay in the future. Stay tuned!
# Wispy Engine
Wispy Engine is the game engine I am creating alongside WayLand. It is in C++ and uses the Windows API, so it is currently only compatible with Windows PCs.
